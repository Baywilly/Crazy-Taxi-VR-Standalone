## 📥 DOWNLOAD & INSTALLATION

Since Crazy Taxi was removed from digital stores by SEGA, finding a way to share our virtual reality adaptation while respecting the original game's intellectual property rights has been complicated.

For this reason, we have chosen to distribute **an XDelta patch** instead of sharing the complete commercial game.

### 1️⃣ Obtain the compatible original file

To use our patch, you will need a legitimate copy of the compatible original game file.

The specific file used to prepare the patch has the following name:

`com.sega.CrazyTaxiplayhaven_6.0-80_2arch_7dpi_24lang_1feat_017e8b1c582569ff9682193551b7e22c_apkmirror.com.apkm`

**IMPORTANT:** The patch is designed for this specific file and may not work with other versions of Crazy Taxi for Android.

The filename is provided as a reference to identify the compatible version. Original game files must be obtained through legal means.

---

### 2️⃣ Download our XDelta patch

In the **Releases** section of this repository, once published, you will be able to download our file:

**`crazywilly.xdelta`**

Download it and save it to your computer.

---

### 3️⃣ Download Delta Patcher

To apply our patch, you will need the free program **Delta Patcher**.

You can find it on its official project page:

https://github.com/marco-calautti/DeltaPatcher

Download the program to your computer and run it.

**The computer is only needed to prepare and install the game. Once installed, Crazy Taxi VR runs directly on your Meta Quest headset.**

---

### 4️⃣ Apply the XDelta patch

Open **Delta Patcher** on your computer.

<img width="270" height="253" alt="aplicacion Xdelta" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2805f83d-98c9-4274-b571-0361b804f35e" />

The application has two main fields:

**📁 Original file**

Select the original Crazy Taxi file:

`com.sega.CrazyTaxiplayhaven_6.0-80_2arch_7dpi_24lang_1feat_017e8b1c582569ff9682193551b7e22c_apkmirror.com.apkm`

**📁 XDelta patch**

Select our patch:

`crazywilly.xdelta`

Then:

1. Select both files in their corresponding fields.
2. Click **Apply patch**.
3. Wait for the patching process to finish.

**Once the process is complete, the original file will have been patched. Rename the file extension from `.apkm` to `.apk`.**

After completing these steps, you should have the Crazy Taxi VR Standalone file ready to install on your Meta Quest headset.

---

### 5️⃣ Install Crazy Taxi VR on Meta Quest

1. Connect your Meta Quest headset to your computer using a USB cable.
2. Make sure Developer Mode and the necessary permissions for installing external applications are enabled.
3. Open **SideQuest** or another compatible installation tool.
4. Select the `.apk` file you just patched.
5. Install it on your headset.
6. Wait for the installation to finish.

Once the installation is complete, you can disconnect the USB cable.

**🚕 DONE! YOU CAN NOW ENJOY CRAZY TAXI VR STANDALONE DIRECTLY ON YOUR HEADSET!**

---

### 6️⃣ Launch Crazy Taxi VR Standalone

Put on your Meta Quest headset and open your app library.

Find **Crazy Taxi VR Standalone** among your installed applications.

Launch the game, select your driver, and get ready to hit the streets.

**🚕 HEY, HEY! IT'S CRAZY TAXI!**

## 🎮 CONTROLS — META QUEST TOUCH

Crazy Taxi VR Standalone includes controls adapted for Meta Quest Touch controllers.

### Left controller

- **Joystick:** steer the taxi left or right.
- **Y button:** previous camera.
- **X button:** next camera.
- **Menu button:** pause.
- **Left trigger:** brake.
- **Left grip:** hold the steering wheel in cockpit view or activate Turbo Boost / Crazy Dash in external view.

### Right controller

- **Right trigger:** accelerate.
- **A button:** forward gear.
- **B button:** reverse gear.
- **Joystick up:** increase 3D depth.
- **Joystick down:** decrease 3D depth.
- **Press right joystick:** recenter the view.
- **Right grip:** hold the steering wheel in cockpit view or activate Turbo Boost / Crazy Drift in external view.
- **Meta button:** headset system menu.

### Menus and character selection

- **Left joystick:** select driver.
- **Right controller and A button:** confirm selection.
- **B button:** go back.

## 🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING

### ❌ Delta Patcher shows an error

Make sure you have selected the exact original file and that the patch has been downloaded correctly.

### ❌ The patched APK does not install

Make sure the patching process completed without errors and that the resulting file is compatible with your headset.

### ❌ The game does not appear on the headset

Check the section for sideloaded or unknown-source applications on your headset.

### ❌ The controllers are not responding

Check the controller mapping image for your language and make sure the controllers are properly connected.

### ❌ The camera is not centered

Press the right joystick to recenter the view.

### ❌ An error occurs during gameplay

You can report it through the **Issues** section of this repository or share your experience with the community.
